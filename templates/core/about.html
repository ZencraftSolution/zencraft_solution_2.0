Why we started Zencraft

Our founders spent years building software abroad and kept meeting the same frustration from businesses at home: projects handed to vendors who disappeared after launch, systems nobody could maintain, and invoices that never matched the original quote.

Zencraft Solution was set up to do the opposite. We publish our rates, write down our decisions, and stay with the software we build. Today, {{ site.stat_engineers }} engineers, designers and QA specialists work from our Jhamsikhel office and across Nepal for clients in 11 countries.

We are proud to be a Nepali company that competes on quality rather than price, and to train the next generation of engineers through our internship program.
