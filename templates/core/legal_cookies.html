This website uses as few cookies as possible and no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Cookies we set

  • csrftoken: protects forms against cross-site request forgery. Required.
  • sessionid: remembers your cart and the confirmation page after you submit a form. Required, expires when the session ends or after 2 weeks.

Local storage

Your currency preference (NPR or USD) is saved in your browser's local storage so prices display the way you chose on your next visit. It never leaves your device.

Third-party content

Fonts, icons, images and animation libraries are loaded from public content delivery networks. These providers may log your IP address for security and performance, under their own policies.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking required cookies will prevent forms and the cart from working.
