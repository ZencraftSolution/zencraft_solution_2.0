{{ site.company_name }} Pvt. Ltd. ("Zencraft", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 of Nepal and, for visitors from the European Union, the principles of the GDPR.

Information we collect

  • Contact and consultation forms: name, email, phone, company and your message.
  • Project estimator: your answers about the project, contact details and any requirements document you upload.
  • Job applications: contact details, CV file or the CV details you enter manually, education and work history, links, salary expectations and notice period.
  • Newsletter: your email address, kept until you ask us to remove it.
  • Orders: billing details and payment status. Card and wallet credentials are handled by the payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Usage data: the page visited, the referring page and a session identifier, recorded without third-party tracking cookies.
  • Technical records: server logs, which include the IP address a request came from. They are also used to limit how often a form can be submitted and to lock out repeated failed sign-ins.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, prepare estimates and proposals, assess job applications, deliver purchased services and improve this website. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

How we protect it

CVs and requirement documents are stored in private storage that is not reachable by a public link. Uploaded files are checked for malware before anyone in the team can open them. Access is limited to staff who need it, requires authentication, and every access, export and erasure is recorded in an audit log. All traffic to this website is encrypted.

How long we keep it

When a record expires we erase the personal details in it and keep an anonymous row, so our own statistics about hiring and enquiries stay accurate. Nothing in that remaining row identifies you.

  • Unsuccessful job applications: personal details and the CV file are erased 6 months after the decision.
  • Project inquiries that do not become projects: 24 months.
  • Order and invoice records: as long as tax law requires, currently 6 years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected, have it erased, or withdraw consent at any time, by writing to {{ site.email }}. Tell us which email address you used so we can find the record. We respond within 30 days, and erasure is carried out straight away rather than waiting for the retention period above.
