{{ site.company_name }} Pvt. Ltd. ("Zencraft", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Individual Privacy Act, 2075 of Nepal and, for visitors from the European Union, the principles of the GDPR.

Information we collect

  • Contact and consultation forms: name, email, phone, company and your message.
  • Project estimator: your answers about the project, contact details and any requirements document you upload.
  • Job applications: contact details, CV file or the CV details you enter manually, links, salary expectations and notice period.
  • Orders: billing details and payment status. Card and wallet credentials are handled by the payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Usage data: pages visited and referring page, recorded without third-party tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, prepare estimates and proposals, assess job applications, deliver purchased services and improve this website. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

How we protect it

CVs and requirement documents are stored in private storage that is not reachable by a public link. Access is limited to staff who need it, requires authentication, and every access is recorded in an audit log. All traffic to this website is encrypted.

How long we keep it

  • Unsuccessful job applications: deleted 6 months after the decision.
  • Project inquiries that do not become projects: 24 months.
  • Order and invoice records: as long as tax law requires, currently 6 years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent at any time, by writing to {{ site.email }}. We respond within 30 days.
